# Institutional Metrics & Configuration Guide

Welcome to the Quantitative Master Engine's official metrics guide. Because this engine is built on institutional-grade financial principles, it strips away the simplified "budget tracker" metrics and relies on true quantitative risk analytics.

This guide will walk you through *every single parameter* available in the `financial_rules.toml` configuration, exactly how the Quant Engine calculates Alpha, and how to interpret the downstream presentation ratios in your dashboards.

---

## ⚙️ 1. Financial Rules Engine (`financial_rules.toml`)

The engine's logic is dynamically driven by your configuration. Adjusting these parameters fundamentally alters how your data is parsed, how your tax-alpha is harvested, and how your FIRE survival probabilities are calculated.

### Structural Mappings
These arrays map raw categories from your extraction sources to institutional tags, ensuring the engine knows how to treat your cashflow.
* **`[income.active]` / `[income.dividends]` / `[income.interest]`**: Maps your raw income strings into active vs. passive buckets. If dividends aren't mapped here, the engine won't correctly calculate your *Organic Yield*.
* **`[expense.core]`**: Maps what you consider "Core" survival expenses (e.g., rent, groceries) vs. discretionary spending. Your FIRE targets are based *exclusively* on core expenses, not discretionary splurges.
* **`[assets.illiquid]`**: Defines assets like Real Estate or locked EPF. The engine strips these out when calculating *Liquid Liability Coverage* and *Months of Runway*.
* **`[investments]`**: Identifies which asset subclasses represent your active investment portfolio for Modified Dietz tracking.
* **`[assumptions.target_allocations]`**: A key-value map defining your target portfolio weights (e.g., `Direct Stocks = 0.40`). The engine computes `Target_Deviation` against these weights.

### Macro Assumptions (`[assumptions.macro]`)
These act as fallbacks if your temporal `d_Macro_Parameters` database dimension is missing data for a specific month.
* **`default_trading_days`** (e.g., `252`): The number of trading days in a year. Used to annualize daily volatility calculations in the Risk Dashboard.
* **`fallback_inflation_rate`** (e.g., `0.06` or 6%): Used to degrade nominal wealth into *Real* purchasing power and compound future FIRE targets.
* **`fallback_risk_free_rate`** (e.g., `0.06` or 6%): The assumed risk-free yield. This directly affects your Sharpe and Sortino ratios by setting the hurdle rate.

### Capital Market Assumptions (`[assumptions.cma]`)
* **`expected_real_return`** (e.g., `0.05` or 5%): The expected real (inflation-adjusted) return of your portfolio. Used as the mean drift in the Monte Carlo simulations.
* **`fat_tail_multiplier`** (e.g., `1.2`): Standard normal distributions fail to predict black swan crashes. This multiplier inflates your historical volatility to simulate "fat tails" (extreme drawdowns) during the Monte Carlo paths.

### Monte Carlo & FIRE Engine (`[assumptions.monte_carlo]` & `[assumptions.fire]`)
* **`swr_multiplier`** (e.g., `25.0` for the 4% rule): Multiplied against your trailing 12M core expenses to calculate your absolute FIRE target.
* **`coast_fi_real_return`** / **`coast_fi_years`**: Calculates Coast FI (the amount you need today so that you never have to invest again). Uses the `coast_fi_real_return` compounding over `coast_fi_years`.
* **`lean_fi_ratio`** (e.g., `0.7`): The percentage of your absolute FIRE target that constitutes a "Lean" or survival-only retirement.
* **`fallback_trailing_return`**: The nominal return used for deterministic forecasting if historical market data is too volatile to use.
* **`iterations`** (e.g., `1000`): The number of independent, simulated future paths generated by the geometric Brownian motion.
* **`max_months`** (e.g., `360`): The simulation horizon (e.g., 30 years).
* **`annual_volatility`** & **`real_return_floor`**: Defines the bounds of the stochastic random walk. The floor prevents the engine from generating impossible negative bounds that break exponential functions.

### Tax-Alpha (`[assumptions.tax]`)
* **`debt_mf_cutoff_date`**: Hardcoded date (e.g., `"2023-04-01"`) where tax rules changed (grandfathering clause for indexation benefits on debt mutual funds).
* **`ltcg_thresholds`**: A map defining holding periods (e.g., `equity = 365`, `debt = 1095`) before an asset transitions from Short-Term to Long-Term.
* **`fallback_equity_ltcg_exemption`**: The annual tax-free exemption limit (e.g., `125000`) for equity LTCG. The engine utilizes this to harvest tax-free step-ups.
* **`harvest_wait_days_threshold`** (e.g., `90`): The minimum wash-sale period.
* **`rates`**: Your specific Long-Term (LTCG) and Short-Term (STCG) tax rates across Equity, Debt, and Gold. The engine uses this to calculate exactly how much fiat currency you save by harvesting a loss.

## 📈 2. Investment Quant Engine Metrics

This layer evaluates your raw portfolio against the broader market. It completely ignores external cash flows (like your salary) to grade your actual investment acumen.

### True Return (Modified Dietz)
* **What it is**: The absolute return of your portfolio, explicitly isolating and neutralizing internal cashflows (`Inv_Cashflow`).
* **Why it matters**: Your nominal growth is distorted by how much you save. Modified Dietz zeroes out your savings rate so you know exactly what the *market* gave you. Compare this against `Benchmark_Return` to calculate true Alpha.

### Tax-Alpha Harvesting Engine (`p_tf_tax_harvesting`)
The engine scans every single open FIFO lot and flags action items:
* **`HARVEST_LOSS`**: The lot is negative. Sell it to offset other gains. The `Net_Tax_Benefit` field calculates the exact fiat currency you will save based on the asset's STCG/LTCG class.
* **`HARVEST_LTCG_EXEMPT`**: The lot has an unrealized LTCG *below* your annual `fallback_equity_ltcg_exemption`. You can sell it and immediately rebuy it to reset your cost basis (tax-free step-up).
* **`WAIT_FOR_LTCG`**: A short-term gain that is about to cross the 1-year mark. The engine warns you *not* to sell it until the threshold is crossed to avoid the STCG penalty.

### Rolling Risk Metrics (`p_tf_risk_metrics`)
* **`Annualized_Volatility_12M`**: The standard deviation of the last 12 months of daily returns, scaled by `sqrt(252)`.
* **Sharpe Ratio (Dynamic)**: `(Rolling_12M_Return - Risk_Free_Rate) / Annualized_Volatility_12M`. Target **> 1.0**.
* **Sortino Ratio**: Penalizes *Downside Volatility* only. Target **> 1.0**.
* **Calmar Ratio**: `Rolling_12M_Return / Max_Drawdown_12M`. Evaluates how painful the ride is compared to the destination. Target **> 0.5**.

### Sector Allocation & Concentration (`p_tf_sector_allocation_monthly`)
* **Concentration Risk (`Benchmark_Deviation`)**: Measures the delta between your actual allocation % and the ideal % defined in `target_allocations`.
* **`Class_HHI_Concentration_Index`**: Herfindahl-Hirschman Index. Measures how monopolized your portfolio is. A high number means you are heavily undiversified.
* **`Is_Overweight`**: A boolean flag warning if a specific sector has drifted dangerously beyond its target benchmark.

## 🏦 3. Core Presentation Metrics (Wealth & Solvency)

The UI/BI ingestion layer. These metrics assess your overall structural financial health, combining your investments with your income, expenses, and liabilities.

### Structural Ratios (`p_tf_Financial_Ratios_Monthly`)
* **`Savings_Rate_%`**: `(Net_Savings / Total_Income)`. Institutional target: **> 40%**.
* **`Debt_to_Asset_Ratio_%`**: `(Total_Liabilities / Total_Assets)`. Institutional target: **< 0.30**.
* **`Liquid_Liability_Coverage_Ratio`**: `Liquid Assets / Total Liabilities`. A strict insolvency stress-test. Demands to know if you can clear your debts *tomorrow* using only liquid capital. Target: **> 1.0**.
* **`Months_of_Runway`**: `Liquid Assets / Trailing 3M Avg Expenses`. Calculates exactly how many months you can survive a total loss of income. Target: **12.0 to 24.0**.

### Growth & Efficiency Metrics (`p_tf_Net_Worth_Monthly_Summary`)
* **Organic Yield %**: `(Surplus/Deficit - Net Cashflow) / Opening Balance`. Measures the pure organic growth of your net worth strictly from investment appreciation and passive interest, isolating out your manual paycheck deposits.
* **Asset Velocity %**: `Total_Invested_Capital / Liquid_Net_Worth`. The speed at which your liquid cash is being deployed into the market. High is good during accumulation phases.
* **Real Drawdown %**: Strips away the "Nominal Illusion." If your portfolio goes up 2% but inflation is 6%, your *Real* Drawdown will flag a -4% loss in purchasing power, even if your nominal account balance hit an all-time high.
* **Category Inflation Trends (MoM & YoY)**: Found in `p_tf_category_spend_analytics`. Compares spending in specific categories against trailing periods to detect personal lifestyle creep or localized hyper-inflation (e.g., rent increases).

### Income Intelligence (`p_tf_income_streams_monthly`)
* **`Income_Diversification_Score`**: Measures how dependent you are on a single stream (e.g., your W2). A higher score indicates a resilient, multi-stream passive income architecture.
* **`Months_Since_Last_Received`**: Tracks the cadence of passive cashflow. Automatically warns you if a dividend or side-hustle stream has gone dormant.

## 🔥 4. Stochastic FIRE Outputs (`p_tf_fire_forecasting_monthly`)

The engine simulates 1,000 parallel realities traversing 30 years into the future using Geometric Brownian Motion and Stagflation drifting.

### Probability Metrics
* **`Probability_of_Success`**: The percentage of Monte Carlo paths where your portfolio survived the max duration without hitting `$0` (Sequence of Return Risk). Target: **> 90%**.
* **`Current_FI_Coverage_Pct`**: `Liquid Assets / FIRE Target`. The raw completion percentage of your FI journey based on today's fiat valuation.

### Stochastic Drifting Targets
* **`Target_FI_Future_Nominal_P50` (50th Percentile)**: Unlike a static spreadsheet, this assumes inflation acts as a volatile Random Walk. It tells you what your FIRE number will physically need to be 10, 20, or 30 years from now just to maintain your current lifestyle.
* **`Wealth_P10`, `Wealth_P50`, `Wealth_P90`**: The projected size of your portfolio across worst-case (P10), median (P50), and best-case (P90) scenarios. 

**Actionable Insight**: If your `Current_FI_Coverage_Pct` is high against today's target, but your future stochastic nominal projections show a massive gap against `Target_FI_Future`, your portfolio is suffering from cash-drag and will be eroded by stagflation. Increase `Asset_Velocity_%` to deploy capital and raise your `expected_real_return`!
