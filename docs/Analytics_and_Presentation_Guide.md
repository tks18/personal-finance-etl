# 🏦 The Quant Engine: Ultimate Analytics & Presentation Layer Guide

This document is the definitive, exhaustive reference for the quantitative analytics and presentation data schemas generated by the personal finance ETL engine. It covers every column, metric, mathematical definition, heuristic (Good vs. Bad), and operational usage across the entire data warehouse.

This is the dictionary that transforms raw transaction data into institutional-grade wealth intelligence.

---

## Part 1: Investment Analytics Engine Layer

The `f_tf_Investment_Analytics_*` tables form the deterministic core of the financial engine. This is where bottom-up, FIFO-based (First-In, First-Out) calculations are performed on every single fractional share you own, rolling up to the portfolio level.

### 1. `f_tf_Investment_Analytics_Lot` (The Atomic Core)
This is the most granular table in the database. Every time you purchase an asset, a unique "Lot" is created. This table tracks the individual risk, return, and tax liability of that specific purchase over time.

#### 📍 Identifiers & Classification
*   **`Closing_Date` (DATE):** The "As-Of" date for the calculated metrics. Used for time-series partitioning.
*   **`ISIN` (TEXT):** Unique International Securities Identification Number. The foreign key to the Investment Master dimension.
*   **`Buy_Date` (DATE):** The exact execution date of the transaction.
*   **`BENCHMARK_ID` (TEXT):** The mapped benchmark (e.g., Nifty50, S&P500) used for relative performance.
*   **`TAX_TYPE` / `TAX_SUBTYPE` (TEXT):** Classification governing the taxation rules for this specific asset (e.g., "Equity_Listed", "Debt_MF").

#### ⏳ Holding Period & Tax Maturity
*   **`Age_Days` (BIGINT):** The absolute number of days the lot has been held since `Buy_Date`.
    *   *Heuristic:* Higher is generally better (indicates lower churn and longer compounding).
*   **`LTCG_Threshold_Days` (BIGINT):** The legally defined holding period required to unlock Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax rates. Sourced from `d_Macro_Parameters`.
*   **`Days_To_LTCG` (BIGINT):** `MAX(0, LTCG_Threshold_Days - Age_Days)`. A countdown timer to tax optimization.
    *   *Usage:* Critical for tax harvesting. If this number is < 30, **DO NOT SELL** unless strictly necessary, as you will incur a massive STCG tax drag.
*   **`Holding_Type` (TEXT):** Evaluates to "STCG" (Short-Term) or "LTCG" (Long-Term). Dictates which tax rate is applied in the liability calculations.
*   **`Dietz_Day_Weight` (DOUBLE):** The fraction of the time period the lot was held. Formula: `(Total_Days_In_Period - Days_Since_Purchase) / Total_Days_In_Period`.
    *   *Usage:* The mathematical foundation of the Modified Dietz Time-Weighted Return, ensuring mid-month buys don't artificially inflate or deflate the return metrics.

#### 💰 Position & Valuation
*   **`Quantity` (DOUBLE):** The number of fractional units/shares held in this specific lot.
*   **`Buy_Price` (DOUBLE):** The unit cost basis at execution.
*   **`Market_Price` (DOUBLE):** The closing price of the asset on `Closing_Date`.
*   **`Buy_Value` (DOUBLE):** `Quantity * Buy_Price`. Your original fiat capital at risk.
*   **`Close_Value` (DOUBLE):** `Quantity * Market_Price`. Mark-to-market current valuation.

#### 📈 Absolute Returns (Lot Level)
*   **`P/L` (DOUBLE):** `Close_Value - Buy_Value`. Raw nominal fiat gained or lost.
*   **`Returns_%` (DOUBLE):** `(Close_Value - Buy_Value) / Buy_Value`. Simple geometric return.
*   **`Lot_Weight_%` (DOUBLE):** What percentage of the *total ISIN position* does this single lot represent?
    *   *Usage:* Helps identify if your cost-basis is heavily skewed by a single poorly timed lump-sum buy.
*   **`Lot_CAGR` (DOUBLE):** Annualized return. Formula: `( (Close_Value/Buy_Value) ^ (365/Age_Days) ) - 1`.
    *   *Heuristic:* Must reliably exceed the current `Inflation_Rate` to generate real wealth.

#### 🏢 Portfolio-Level Returns Mapped to Lot
*   **`CAGR` / `XIRR` (DOUBLE):** The annualized return and Internal Rate of Return of the *parent ISIN*, stamped onto the lot for comparative context.
*   **`After_Tax_XIRR` (DOUBLE):** The XIRR calculated after subtracting the `LTCG_Tax_If_Sold` or `STCG_Tax_If_Sold`.
    *   *Heuristic:* A massive divergence between XIRR and After_Tax_XIRR indicates extreme tax inefficiency (heavy STCG exposure).

#### 🥊 Benchmark Comparison & Alpha
*   **`BM_Buy_Price` / `BM_Market_Price` (DOUBLE):** Reconstructs the exact value if you had bought the benchmark on the same day instead of the asset.
*   **`Lot_BM_Returns_%` / `Lot_BM_CAGR` / `BM_CAGR` / `BM_XIRR` (DOUBLE):** Corresponding returns of the benchmark.
*   **`Active_Return` (DOUBLE):** `Asset_XIRR - BM_XIRR`. The absolute percentage points you beat (or lost to) the market.
*   **`Lot_Alpha` (DOUBLE):** The active return of this specific lot. Identifies whether this exact entry point was good market timing.
    *   *Heuristic:* > 0 is Alpha (Skill/Luck). < 0 is Value Destruction.
*   **`Is_Lagging_Benchmark` (BIGINT):** Boolean `1` if `Active_Return < 0`. Primary filter for the Tax Loss Harvesting AI.

#### ⚖️ Risk Metrics (Modern Portfolio Theory)
*   **`Beta` (DOUBLE):** A measure of volatility relative to the benchmark.
    *   *Heuristic:* `< 1` (Defensive, moves less than market), `> 1` (Aggressive, amplified moves).
*   **`Tracking_Error` (DOUBLE):** Standard deviation of the `Active_Return` over time. How wildly the asset diverges from the index.
*   **`Information_Ratio` (DOUBLE):** `Active_Return / Tracking_Error`.
    *   *Usage:* The ultimate measure of a fund manager. Are you getting paid for taking non-index risk?
    *   *Heuristic:* > 0.5 is Good. > 1.0 is Exceptional. < 0 means you are taking risk to lose money.
*   **`Upside_Capture` / `Downside_Capture` (DOUBLE):** How much of the benchmark's positive (or negative) returns the asset captures.
    *   *Heuristic:* Asymmetry is king. You want Upside > 100 and Downside < 100.

#### 🏛️ Tax Exposure (The Tax Alpha Engine)
*   **`Tax_Rate` (DOUBLE):** The dynamic percentage rate pulled from `d_Macro_Parameters` based on `TAX_TYPE` and `Holding_Type`.
*   **`Unrealized_LTCG` / `Unrealized_STCG` / `Unrealized_Loss` (DOUBLE):** Deconstructs the raw `P/L` into its tax buckets.
*   **`LTCG_Tax_If_Sold` / `STCG_Tax_If_Sold` (DOUBLE):** The exact fiat bill you will owe the government if you execute a sell order right now.
*   **`After_Tax_PL` / `After_Tax_Close_Value` (DOUBLE):** Mark-to-market value strictly net of liabilities. Your *true* liquid wealth.

#### 💼 Portfolio Aggregates & Forecasting
*   **`Portfolio_Weight_%` (DOUBLE):** The percentage of your entire Net Worth tied up in this single lot.
*   **`Portfolio_XIRR` / `Portfolio_After_Tax_XIRR` / `Portfolio_Sharpe_Ratio` / `Max_Drawdown`:** Macro-level metrics appended here for cross-filtering.
*   **`Outperformance_Probability` (DOUBLE):** Using historical Monte Carlo simulations, the statistical probability this lot beats the benchmark going forward.
    *   *Heuristic:* > 60% confidence is a "Hold". < 40% confidence warrants a "Sell/Harvest".

#### 🎯 Tax Harvesting Signals (Actionable AI)
*   **`FY_Realized_*` (DOUBLE):** Running totals for the current Financial Year to track against exemption limits.
*   **`Stepup_Eligible` (BIGINT):** Boolean flag. Is the lot grandfathered into a tax-free step-up?
*   **`Can_Harvest_Loss` (BOOLEAN):** Is this lot in the red, and do we have gains elsewhere to offset?
*   **`Harvest_Recommendation` (TEXT):** Plain English output (e.g., "HOLD - Approaching LTCG", "SELL - Harvest STCL"). Used directly in the BI dashboard.

### 2. The Rollup Layers (`ISIN`, `Subtype`, `Class`, `Portfolio`)
These tables aggregate the atomic Lot data using volume-weighted math up the dimensional hierarchy.
*   **`f_tf_Investment_Analytics_ISIN`:** Rolled up to the individual asset (e.g., Apple Inc, Vanguard S&P500). Provides `Total_Invested_Value` and `Total_Current_Value`.
*   **`f_tf_Investment_Analytics_Subtype`:** Aggregates by asset subtype (e.g., "Large Cap Equity", "Corporate Bonds"). Introduces `Sharpe_Ratio` and `Sortino_Ratio` at the asset-class level.
*   **`f_tf_Investment_Analytics_Class`:** High-level splits (Equity vs Debt vs Gold vs Real Estate). Used to monitor macro risk-premiums.
*   **`f_tf_Investment_Analytics_Portfolio`:** The master level. Your entire invested net worth represented in a single row per `Closing_Date`.

## Part 2: Presentation Layer (`p_tf_*`)

The `p_tf` (Presentation) schemas are aggressively pre-aggregated, highly polished tables materialized directly into DuckDB for instant frontend BI consumption.

### 1. `p_tf_Net_Worth_Monthly_Summary` (The Master Snapshot)
Tracks the absolute fiat accumulation and organic momentum of wealth.

*   **`Opening_Balance` / `Closing_Balance` (REAL):** Book-value of assets. (Money In vs Money Out).
*   **`Closing_Balance_Market` (REAL):** Mark-to-market value including all unrealized gains. **This is your true Net Worth.**
*   **`All_Time_High_Balance` (REAL) & `Drawdown_From_Peak` (REAL):** Essential for psychological risk management. Displays how far you are from peak wealth.
*   **`Liquid_Assets` (REAL):** Cash and near-cash equivalents. The denominator for survival runways.
*   **`Income_Inflow` / `Expense_Outflow` / `Net_Cashflow_Month` (REAL):** Total capital pipelines.
*   **`Core_Expense_Outflow` (REAL):** Hard survival expenses (rent, groceries). Excludes discretionary spending. Core to Lean FI math.
*   **`MoM_Balance_Growth_%` / `YoY_Balance_Growth_%` (REAL):** Velocity of capital accumulation.
*   **`Organic_Growth_Value` / `Organic_Yield_%` (REAL):** `Total Market Growth - Net Deposits`. Isolates the actual money your investments made for you, completely stripped of your salary deposits.
    *   *Heuristic:* If this is consistently negative while Net Worth grows, you are saving heavily but investing poorly.
*   **`Asset_Velocity_%` (REAL):** The rate at which the portfolio is generating cashflow relative to its size.
*   **`Savings_to_NW_Ratio` (REAL):** Monthly savings / Total Net Worth.
    *   *Heuristic:* Early in accumulation, this is high. Later on, market returns dwarf your salary, and this number collapses. A key indicator of late-stage FIRE.
*   **`Months_of_Runway` (REAL):** `Liquid_Assets / 3M_Avg_Expense`.
    *   *Heuristic:* < 3 (Danger), 3-6 (Stable), > 12 (Highly conservative).
*   **`*_Real` Metrics:** E.g., `Closing_Balance_Real`. Adjusted for `INFLATION_YOY_PCT`. Eradicates the illusion of nominal growth.

### 2. `p_tf_Financial_Ratios_Monthly` (Corporate Finance for the Household)
*   **`Savings_Rate_%` (REAL):** The apex metric for FIRE. `Net Cashflow / Total Income`.
    *   *Heuristic:* > 20% is solid. > 50% guarantees early retirement.
*   **`Income_Surplus_Rate_%` (REAL):** `(Income - Core Expenses) / Income`. Your true margin of safety against job loss or pay cuts.
*   **`Net_Worth_to_Annual_Expense_Ratio` (REAL):** The inverse of the Safe Withdrawal Rate. At 25x, you are FI.
*   **`Debt_to_Asset_Ratio_%` (REAL):** Total Liabilities / Total Assets. Leverage check. Should remain under 30% ideally.
*   **`Liquid_Liability_Coverage_Ratio` (REAL):** Liquid Assets / Total Liabilities. Can you pay off all debt tomorrow if required?

### 3. Spend & Income Analytics (`p_tf_category_spend_analytics`, `p_tf_income_streams_monthly`)
*   **`Spend_Consistency_Score` / `Income_Stability_Score` (REAL):** Inverse of rolling standard deviation.
    *   *Heuristic:* You want high consistency in core expenses (predictability) and high stability in income.
*   **`Income_Diversification_Score` (REAL):** Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) applied to income. Protects against single-employer risk.
*   **`Is_Category_Creep` (BOOLEAN):** AI-flag indicating if Trailing 3M spend exceeds Trailing 12M average by a statistically significant margin (Lifestyle Inflation alert).

### 4. `p_tf_fire_forecasting_monthly` (The Institutional GOAT Engine)
Runs deterministic and stochastic (Merton Jump-Diffusion) simulations to predict Financial Independence.

*   **`Target_FI_Today` vs `Target_FI_Future_Nominal`:** The fiat required to retire right now, versus the massively inflated fiat number you will need in the actual year you hit the goal.
*   **`Coast_FI_Today`:** The number needed *today* such that it will grow to `Target_FI` at retirement age without a single additional dollar of savings.
*   **`Lean_FI_Today`:** FI based purely on `Core_Expense_Outflow` (survival mode).
*   **`Estimated_Months_To_FI_*` (P90, P50, P10):** Monte Carlo percentiles. P90 (Conservative) assumes a brutal market environment. P10 (Aggressive) assumes a multi-year bull run.
*   **`Probability_Of_Success_Pct`:** The definitive confidence score. % of Monte Carlo pathways that didn't go bankrupt.
    *   *Heuristic:* Target > 95%.
*   **`Wealth_Velocity` & `Wealth_Acceleration`:** First and second derivatives of Net Worth over time. Is the compounding compounding?
*   **`CAPE_Adjusted_SWR` (DOUBLE):** Dynamically alters the 4% rule based on the Shiller P/E ratio. If markets are overvalued, this drops to 3.2% to prevent sequence of return risk.
*   **`Guyton_Klinger_Floor` / `Ceiling`:** Dynamic spending boundaries for decumulation. Tells you exactly when to take a pay cut or give yourself a bonus in retirement based on portfolio performance.
*   **`Human_Capital_Value`:** The Net Present Value (NPV) of your remaining lifetime earnings capacity.

### 5. `p_tf_risk_metrics` & `p_tf_sector_allocation_monthly` (Prime Brokerage Risk)
*   **`Expected_Shortfall_95` (DOUBLE):** CVaR. The average expected loss in the worst 5% of months. Far superior to basic VaR which ignores fat-tail severity.
*   **`Annualized_Volatility_12M` & `Downside_Deviation_12M`:** Separates "good" volatility (upside) from "bad" volatility (crashing).
*   **`Calmar_Ratio`:** `Annualized Return / Max Drawdown`. The gold standard for hedge fund return-to-risk evaluation.
*   **`Marginal_Risk_Contribution` (DOUBLE):** How much volatility adding $1 to a sector adds to the total portfolio variance.
    *   *Usage:* A portfolio split 50/50 Equity/Bonds does NOT have 50/50 risk. Equities might contribute 90% of the total risk. MRC allows for true Risk Parity rebalancing.
*   **`Effective_Diversification`:** `1 - HHI Index`. Measures true capital spread.

### 6. `p_tf_performance_attribution` (Brinson-Fachler Model)
*   **`Allocation_Effect` (DOUBLE):** The return generated strictly by overweighting/underweighting a sector relative to the benchmark. (e.g., Being heavy in Tech when Tech outperforms).
*   **`Selection_Effect` (DOUBLE):** The return generated strictly by picking better assets *within* a sector. (e.g., Picking Nvidia over Intel).
*   **`Interaction_Effect` (DOUBLE):** The cross-product of allocation and selection.
*   **`Total_Active_Return` (DOUBLE):** The sum of the above. Pure, untainted alpha.

### 7. `p_tf_tax_harvesting` (Actionable TLH Output)
*   **`Harvestable_Loss` (DOUBLE):** Available losses ready to be realized.
*   **`Net_Tax_Benefit` (REAL):** Actual fiat saved if you hit "Sell" today.
*   **`Substitute_Asset_Available` (BOOLEAN):** True if you can rotate into a correlated asset (avoiding wash sales while maintaining market exposure).
*   **`Priority_Score` (REAL):** A ranked score combining the magnitude of the loss, the time remaining until it becomes a less valuable Long-Term loss, and the availability of substitutes. The ultimate dashboard signal for the user to execute trades.
